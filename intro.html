Introduction

Why are viruses not considered living?

Viruses are not considered living because of their lack of organelles and the fact that they rely on a host cell to create copies of themselves. Viruses are not self-supportive and must use an external cell to replicate. Living cells are more complex and have organelles that support the cell in replication.

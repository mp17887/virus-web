Polio Structure

Polio has an icosahedral structure and uses RNA as its genetic material. This diagram shows the icosahedral shape and the RNA as genetic material. The icosahedral shape can be identified because of the triangular shape, on a 3-d figure it would be visible how the traingular shapes make up the virus particle's circular shape. The RNA is labeled on the diagram as ssRNA, after further research the RNA is a positive strand RNA virus.

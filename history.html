Treatment

Currently there is no cure to polio, the main thing that can be done is to get vaccinated. Once someone is vaccinated, they are more likely to stay immune to polio because their immune system knows how to combat it. This is why children are so susceptible to it, if they are not vaccinated properly, their body does not know how to defend against it.
There are two different types of vaccines, an oral one and an inactive one. The inactive vaccine is most commonly used and contains "dead" poliovirus, it is given in a series of shots. The oral vaccine is used in areas where polio still naturally occurs, it is a liquid that is swallowed, it contains a live weakened polio virus.
Once someone gets polio, if they are affected strongly, they may need ventilation machines to assist breathing or physical therapy to help their limbs. For people who are less affected and have more common/less serious symptoms, they can take pain relievers, use heat packs to help muscle aches, drink fluids, and get rest.
